# Piperoom product vision, October 2026: the incident room nobody can beat on experience

Written by the Fable lead after three independent reviews of `main` @ 998c987 (an on-call engineer's
scripted walkthrough with 81 screenshots, a principal designer's critique against the ui-v2 boards, and a
platform/agent strategist's capability audit). The full reports live with the orchestration session;
the parts that matter for building are here.

## 1. Where we stand

**What is already best in class and must not be broken:** the approval card (steps, layer badges, params
with `target` flags, fingerprint, per-layer identity, "your approval is void if the steps change"); the
keyboard model and ⌘K; login with layer badges; a11y hygiene (0 unlabeled controls, deliberate `aria-live`);
~100 ms presence fan-out; the event-sourced reducer and the integrity-hashed export; the trust plumbing
(read-only tools, untrusted-text wrappers, injection scan, redaction, `params_hash` executor,
verify-after-fix, shadow mode, evals); light/dark tokens that match the boards.

**The honest verdict:** the room still reads like an event log wearing a nice coat. The agent is
trustworthy in code but not *visibly* trustworthy on screen; the product's decisive moments (approve,
hand off, fix ran) are either below the fold, invisible, or silent.

## 2. Five principles we own

1. **The decision is always above the fold.** Whatever needs *you* is the first thing on screen, on any device.
2. **Every sentence has a receipt.** A claim the agent makes links to the step and the exact query that backs it. Unbacked claims are marked.
3. **The room tells you what is happening, not what happened.** One derived status, one curated feed, one attention system.
4. **The fix is a ceremony with a payoff.** Blast radius before, visible execution during, verification after, close nudge at the end.
5. **The room survives people.** Hand-off summaries are pinned, catch-up is automatic, post-mortems draft themselves.

## 5. Three signature interactions (what people will talk about)

- **The Evidence Trail.** Hover a word in the hypothesis and the step that proves it lights up; click and you see the query and can copy it. No competitor does this.
- **The Approval Ceremony.** Four people agree and you watch it happen: presence on each pending row, the blast radius under the steps, the Registry's own dry-run verdict, then the execution sequence and the verification card, then "Close room".
- **Shift Change.** The room hands itself over: the incoming driver reads the pinned summary before accepting, and the post-mortem drafts itself on close.

## 6. What we deliberately do not build yet

`logs_search` (needs a pilot's backend and its own design), signed Slack approvals (identity binding design first), cross-pipeline storm correlation, BigQuery/Databricks warehouse kinds, mobile push. All are in the platform review with designs; they come after the F wave proves trust.
